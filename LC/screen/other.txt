 Other commands:

 >VIEW MONITOR
 To toggle on AND off the main monitor's map cam.

 >SWITCH
 To switch view to a player on the main monitor.

 >PING
 To make a radar booster play a noise.

 >TRANSMIT
 To transmit a message with the signal translator.

 >SCAN
 To scan for the number of items left on the current
 moon.
